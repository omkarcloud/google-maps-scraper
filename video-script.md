# Video Script — "How I Got 10,000 Leads From Google Maps For $0"

**Target length:** ~4–5 min · **SAY** = spoken word-for-word · **SHOW** = on screen

**Title options (for CTR):**
1. I Scraped 10,000 Leads From Google Maps For $0 (Live)
2. Get 100,000 Free Leads From Google Maps (No Coding)
3. Lead Sellers Charge $400 For This. I'll Do It Free.

---

## 1. COLD OPEN — Proof first (0:00–0:15)

**SAY:** This is a list of ten thousand restaurants in New York City. Name, phone number, website, rating, address — everything. It took 20 minutes to build, and it cost me zero dollars.

**SHOW:** Fast scroll through the finished spreadsheet. Zoom on row counter: `10,047`.

**SAY:** Companies like Apify charge around $400 for a list this size. Today you'll build it yourself for free — and stay till the end, because I'll also show you how to get an **email address for every single lead**. Also free.

**SHOW:** Apify pricing page → big red ❌ → text: **$400 → $0**

> 🎯 Retention: result shown before anything else. No "hey guys, welcome back". The email promise is the open loop that holds people to the end.

---

## 2. THE TOOL — 8 seconds of credibility (0:15–0:30)

**SAY:** I'm using my Google Maps Extractor app. It's free, it has 2,800 stars, and it's rated Excellent on Trustpilot. The whole process is three steps — watch.

**SHOW:** Quick cuts, 1–2s each: app page → star count → Trustpilot "Excellent" widget.

> 🎯 Say "rated Excellent on Trustpilot" — don't say "by 21 users" out loud, just show the widget. Don't linger here; credibility over 10 seconds feels like an ad.

---

## 3. LIVE DEMO — Setup (0:30–1:05)

**SAY:** Step one — install it. Link in the description.

**SAY:** Step two — type what you want and where. I'm typing **Restaurants in New York City**. You could type gyms in London, dentists in Dallas, yoga studios in Mumbai — anything that's on Google Maps, you can have as a lead list.

**SAY:** Step three — hit Search. That's it. That's the entire skill.

**SHOW:** Typing → click **Search** → first results start streaming in live.

> 🎯 Show rows appearing within seconds — motion on screen right after the click.

---

## 4. THE WAIT — Never show a loading bar (1:05–1:50)

**SAY:** Ten thousand leads takes about 20 minutes. While it runs, let me show you the feature that actually makes you money — **filters**.

**SAY:** Doing cold calls? Filter to businesses **with** phone numbers. Selling websites? Filter to businesses **without** a website — every single one of those is a client waiting to happen. Want serious buyers? Minimum 4 stars, 50-plus reviews. You sort and filter until the list is exactly your customer.

**SHOW:** Filter panel demo on an existing list — toggle "has phone", "no website", ratings slider.

> 🎯 This fills dead time with the strongest selling point. Filters = "this tool thinks like a salesperson", which is what converts viewers into users.

---

## 5. PAYOFF (1:50–2:30)

**SAY:** Twenty minutes later — done. Ten thousand and twenty-four restaurants.

**SHOW:** *[ANIM: clock spin]* → results table → zoom the count.

**SAY:** Phone numbers, websites, addresses, ratings, review counts. Look at this.

**SHOW:** Slow scroll across columns — let the data breathe for a few seconds.

**SAY:** One click — **Export as CSV** — and it opens in Excel or Google Sheets. This list is ready for cold calls *today*.

**SHOW:** Click Export → CSV opens in Sheets.

---

## 6. PRICING — After they've seen the value (2:30–3:15)

**SAY:** So what does this cost? This is the part nobody believes.

**SAY:** The free plan gives you **200 searches**. One search just got me ten thousand leads. Do the math — that's room for over **one hundred thousand leads**, on the free plan. Apify charges four hundred dollars for that. Here, it's free.

**SHOW:** Text overlays stacking: `200 free searches` → `1 search = 10K leads` → `100K+ leads free` → `Apify: $400 ❌`

**SAY:** So honestly — most of you should never pay me. For 99% of people, the free plan is all you'll ever need.

> 🎯 "You should never pay me" is the biggest trust spike in the video. Keep it word-for-word.

**SAY:** If you're an agency and you actually burn through 200 searches — sixteen dollars gets you five thousand searches, forty-eight gets you unlimited. That's the whole pricing page.

**SAY (quick, one breath):** Oh — and if you're a college student, Pro is completely free for you. I'll show you how in the next video.

**SHOW:** `$16 → 5,000 searches` / `$48 → Unlimited` · then `🎓 Students: Pro FREE` card.

---

## 7. THE PROMISED PAYOFF — Emails (3:15–3:45)

**SAY:** Now, the thing I promised you. Cold email needs emails — and Google Maps doesn't show them. So use the built-in enrichment: it takes your list, and finds the emails. Free.

**SHOW:** *(screen flash)* Run enrichment → email column filling in, row by row.

> 🎯 This closes the loop from the hook. People who skipped ahead land here — make this moment visually satisfying.

---

## 8. END — Bridge, don't beg (3:45–4:05)

**SAY:** You now know how to pull more leads in 20 minutes than most agencies buy in a year. In the next video, I show the exact search strategy — how to pick keywords, stack filters, and export a clean, ready-to-sell list. Ten minutes, and you'll squeeze every last lead out of this tool. It's on your screen right now.

**SHOW:** End screen with next-video card. Point at it. Hard cut — no outro music fade.

> 🎯 No "like and subscribe" speech — it kills retention at the end and the algorithm reads that drop. Send them to the next video instead; session time beats a like.

---

## Retention rules this script follows

1. **Proof before promise** — the finished list is frame one.
2. **Price anchor early** ($400) so "free" has weight all video.
3. **Open loop in the hook** (free emails) — resolved only at 3:15.
4. **Never show real-time waiting** — the 20-min gap is filled with filters (value + soft pitch).
5. **Pricing only after the payoff** — they've already seen 10K leads, so $0 feels unbelievable and $16/$48 feel trivial.
6. **Trust spike** — "most of you should never pay me."
7. **End bridges to the next video** — no outro, no begging.
